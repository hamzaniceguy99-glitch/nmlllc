Our services, in detail

Four security services, all delivered by licensed officers under written post orders.

Program 01 · Present and visible

Static guarding

Deterrence is most of the value, and deterrence requires being visible and alert rather than seated behind a desk looking at a phone. Supervisors check shifts unannounced, and we would rather you heard about a lapse from us than discovered it on camera.

Program 02 · Verifiable, not claimed

Mobile patrols

The chronic problem with patrols is proving they happened. Electronic checkpoints timestamp each visit and the report goes to you, so a patrol is evidence rather than an invoice line. Timing is varied deliberately, because a predictable patrol is a timetable for anyone watching.

  • Multiple sites
  • Per patrol

Program 03 · Materials and access

Construction site security

Construction theft is usually organised and informed — somebody knew what was delivered and when. Controlling and logging access, and varying patrol timing, removes most of that advantage. We also document the site condition at handover so disputes have a baseline.

Program 04 · Numbers that match the crowd

Event cover

Event security is staffed to the guest count and the venue layout, not to a budget. We will tell you when the number you have asked for is too low — an understaffed entrance at a busy event is how incidents start, and that is a conversation worth having beforehand.
